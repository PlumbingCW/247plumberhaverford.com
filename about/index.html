What Haverford 24/7 Plumbing is

Haverford 24/7 Plumbing is a local emergency plumbing line for Haverford, PA and the Main Line, based at 354 W Lancaster Ave. We answer the phone at every hour and send licensed, insured plumbers to homes and small businesses in Haverford Township, Havertown, Ardmore, Bryn Mawr, Wynnewood, Broomall and Drexel Hill.

We built the service around the call. Someone with a burst pipe or a backed-up drain needs a calm voice, a few clear instructions and a straight price, so that is what the line is set up to give.

How a job is handled

When you call, the dispatcher asks what you see and where you are, then tells you what to shut off. A licensed plumber is sent for a diagnosis. The price is given before the repair begins, and any change in scope is explained and approved before work continues.

We do not publish individual technician names or license numbers on this site, because the line works with several licensed contractors and the plumber who arrives is the one best placed for your address. Ask the plumber at your door for his or her Township license and Pennsylvania contractor registration, and expect a plain answer.

What we do not do

We do not give a price over the phone for a problem nobody has seen, because that price would be a guess. We do not promise an arrival window, because traffic, weather and other emergencies change it. We do not take on work outside plumbing, such as electrical repair or structural drying, and we say so when you call.

We also tell you when a problem may not need an emergency visit. A dripping faucet at midnight can wait until morning if you close the shutoff under the sink, and a good dispatcher will say so.

How the line works

When you call (610) 672-7601, a live dispatcher answers, asks for your address and what is happening and tells you what to shut off. A licensed plumber is then sent to you, and the price is stated before work starts.

We run this as a dispatch line serving Haverford and the Main Line. That means the plumber who arrives is a local professional, and you deal with one number instead of searching for a company that answers at night.

How we handle prices

A plumber cannot price a burst pipe or a stopped sewer line without seeing it. So the price is given on site, after the plumber looks, and before any work starts. You can say no at that point and owe nothing for the work you did not approve.

We do not promise an arrival time, because traffic and weather are outside our control. We do offer same-day appointments when they are available, and the dispatcher tells you honestly what to expect.

Checking any plumber's license

Haverford Township says plumbing work is performed by contractors licensed with the Township and inspected for compliance with the Pennsylvania Uniform Construction Code. That is a useful test for any plumber you hire, ours or someone else's.

Ask for proof of the Township license and ask the Building and Codes Department at 610-446-1000 how to verify it. A plumber who is happy to answer is usually a plumber who is licensed.

What we do not do

We do not use web forms, because a form cannot tell you how to stop water that is already running. We do not post reviews or ratings that we cannot back up. We do not list personal names or license numbers on this site, because plumbers are dispatched from the field.

What we can offer is a phone that answers at any hour and a plain explanation of what is going on in your house.

Where the work happens in Haverford Township

Haverford Township is home to Havertown, Brookline, Beechwood, Oakmont, Manoa, Llanerch and Chatham Park, and its housing ranges from early rail-line neighborhoods to postwar developments. Older houses tend to bring older pipe, so a call from one of those streets often involves a camera look or a water heater near the end of its life.

Knowing that mix helps the dispatcher ask better questions. When you describe the age of the house and where the trouble is, the plumber can bring the right parts on the first trip.

Which services people call about most

Water heater failures, clogged drains and leaks lead the list. They are followed by sewer backups, sump pump failures and burst pipes in winter. Our service pages explain each one in plain language, with the emergency steps first.

If you are not sure which page fits, call. The dispatcher will point you to the right help and, if it is not an emergency, book a time that works.

Where we work

The home area is Haverford Township, including Havertown, Brookline, Beechwood, Oakmont, Manoa, Llanerch, Chatham Park and Bon Air. Five nearby towns have their own pages: Ardmore, Bryn Mawr, Wynnewood, Broomall and Drexel Hill.

You can see every offering on the plumbing services page, or reach us any time through the contact page.
