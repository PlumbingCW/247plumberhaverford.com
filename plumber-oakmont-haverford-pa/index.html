Oakmont, the township's busy middle

Oakmont is an unincorporated community in Haverford Township, and the township's long-range plan describes it as centered on the crossing of Eagle Road and Darby Road. Reference sources describe its edges as running roughly along Campbell Avenue, Winton Avenue, East Marthart Avenue and Darby Road. The 2020 census counted 4,569 people here.

Boundaries here are loose, and the township itself says discernible neighborhood lines are gone. If your address is near Eagle and Darby, this is the page that fits.

A little history that helps

A developer began laying out Oakmont in the 1920s, and a section known as Hillcrest Lawns had become Oakmont by 1925. The Oakmont Fire Company dates to 1912. A Pennsy Trail runs through, on an old rail line.

The township plan also says the buildings along Eagle Road date from around the 1950s. That points to shops and offices with aging restrooms and floor drains. It is a reason to ask about upkeep, not a claim about any one building.

If a drain backs up in a shop or home

Take these steps while you wait for a call back.

Stop using fixtures

Water sent down a blocked line has nowhere to go and may surface at the lowest drain.

Look for the cleanout

Know where the cleanout cap sits, and do not open it if sewage is already rising.

Keep people and stock clear

Move food, paper stock and electronics away from the wet floor.

Note what changed

A new smell, a gurgle or a slow drain that started this week helps the plumber narrow the cause.

If it is a shop, read the page on sewer line cleaning so you know what a cable or jet visit involves.
