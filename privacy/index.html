What this policy covers

This policy explains how Haverford 24/7 Plumbing handles information when you visit 247plumberhaverford.com or call (610) 672-7601. This site has no forms, no accounts and no checkout. The main way we receive information from you is when you call.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start and we will note it.

Information we receive

When you call, we may receive your phone number, your name, your address and a description of the plumbing problem, so that a plumber can reach you. We use that information to dispatch, to follow up on the job and to improve the service.

Like most websites, this one may record basic technical data such as pages visited, browser type and approximate location from the network address. That data helps us keep the site working.

Sharing and retention

Your information is shared with the plumber assigned to your job and with service providers that help us run the phone line and the site. We do not sell your personal information.

We keep call records and job details only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, and then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices and contact

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Email contact@247plumberhaverford.com or write to 354 W Lancaster Ave, Haverford, PA 19041.

We may update this policy from time to time, and the date at the bottom of the page shows the latest revision.
