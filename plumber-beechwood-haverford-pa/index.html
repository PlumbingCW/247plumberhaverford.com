Beechwood, beside the Brookline station

Beechwood is a neighborhood in Haverford Township that appears on 1909 atlas maps. The township's neighborhood list includes the Beechwood subdivision and a townhouse development inside it. Its name is tied to the Beechwood-Brookline station on the SEPTA M Line.

An amusement park called Beechwood Park opened here in 1907 and closed after a few seasons, and only a concrete support remains near the station. Mail uses the Havertown name and ZIP 19083.

Townhouses and single houses

Reference sources describe large brick tract developments in Beechwood, and the township says about three quarters of its housing is single-unit, with about a third of those attached. A townhouse shares walls and sometimes lines, which changes how a leak spreads.

If you live in a townhouse, learn where your own shutoff is. A leak that starts next door can reach you, and a plumber can only isolate what is within reach.

If a pipe leaks in a shared wall

These steps buy time.

  1. Find your shutoffIt is often near where the water line enters or at the meter.
  2. Stop water at the fixtureTurn off the fixture valve under the sink or behind the toilet if you can reach it safely.
  3. Tell the neighborWater may be moving through a shared wall or floor.
  4. Call for helpDescribe the stain, the sound and which rooms are involved.

Sewer pipe and who owns it

Haverford Township's Code places the sewer service lateral with the property owner. Ask a neighbor in a connected row whether the line is shared before you spend money on a repair. The Building and Codes Department at 610-446-1000 can say who needs a permit.
