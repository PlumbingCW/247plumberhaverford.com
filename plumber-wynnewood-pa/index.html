Seasons and the Wynnewood plumbing year

Wynnewood is a quiet Main Line community of older houses on tree-lined streets, and its plumbing calls follow the seasons. Winter brings frozen lines in exterior walls. Spring brings sump pump failures and wet basements. Summer brings sewer backups from roots and stressed lines, and fall brings water heaters that quit when the heat comes on.

Knowing which season is coming tells you what to check now. A pump test in March, a garden hose disconnect in November and a camera look in June all cost less than the emergency they prevent.

Older Wynnewood houses and what they need

Many Wynnewood homes are older, with mature trees, original drain lines and basements that have been finished and refinished over the decades. Roots and old joints are a typical cause of slow drains, and a finished basement raises the cost of a leak.
