Rowhomes, twins and the pipes inside them

Drexel Hill is a community of older rowhomes and twin houses, with many built close together. Shared walls and shared sewer paths are a plumbing fact. A backup in one house can be the symptom of a blocked line that serves several, and a leak in one wall can show up next door.

That is why a plumber here asks about the neighbors. If the house next door also has slow drains, the cause is likely in a shared line, and the fix may involve the Township or the neighbor.

Shared walls, shared risks

In rowhomes and twins, a leak can travel through a shared wall to the neighbor's house. If you smell dampness or see a stain on a party wall, tell the neighbor right away. A quick shutoff and a plumber visit keep a small problem from becoming a dispute.

Keep the neighbor's phone number handy and know where each of your main shutoffs sits. When something goes wrong, minutes count.
