Where Brookline sits inside the township

Brookline is an unincorporated community in Haverford Township, Delaware County. Local histories describe it as running roughly between Mill Road and Manoa Road, with Darby Road and Earlington Road on the other two sides. Brookline Boulevard is the wide central street, and it meets Darby Road in a T.

Darby Road carries the shops, the library, the fire company and the high school. The Beechwood-Brookline station on the SEPTA M Line is the nearest rail stop. Mail here carries the Havertown address and the ZIP 19083, so a Brookline street can look like Havertown on a utility bill.

How the neighborhood was built

Brookline was advertised in 1907 as a new neighborhood, and it appears on 1909 atlas maps. Published histories say it was a streetcar suburb that filled in mostly between then and the early 1930s. The 2020 census counted about 830 housing units here.

That history tells a plumber to expect houses that are roughly a century old. It does not say what is inside any one wall. Some of those houses have had their pipes replaced, and some have not, which is why a first look matters more than a guess.

Who owns the sewer pipe to the street

Under the Haverford Township Code, the sewer service lateral is the property of the customer, and the Township says it is generally not liable for sewer, drain or sump damage. Coverage for a backup is usually an endorsement on a homeowners policy.

The Building and Codes Department is at 610-446-1000 if you need to ask about a permit. Ask your insurance agent about backup coverage before you need it, and ask any plumber to show you the camera footage.
