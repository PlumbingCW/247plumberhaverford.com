Haverford, Havertown and the towns around them

Our dispatch area starts in Haverford, PA 19041 and Havertown 19083 and reaches five nearby communities that each have their own page. The towns sit within a few miles of the Lancaster Avenue address, so a single crew route can serve several of them in a day.

Straight-line distances from Haverford's ZIP 19041 to the others run from about one mile (Ardmore and Bryn Mawr) to about four miles (Drexel Hill). Real drive times depend on traffic, so we quote none. What matters for you is that all five are inside our normal emergency dispatch area.

Which office handles permits where

The town you live in is not always the municipality that issues your permit. Haverford Township handles addresses inside its borders through its Building and Codes Department at 610-446-1000. Part of Ardmore, Wynnewood and Bryn Mawr sits in Lower Merion Township, which has its own office.

Broomall is in Marple Township, and Drexel Hill is in Upper Darby Township. When a job needs a permit, the plumber confirms the right office by your street address and tells you who files it.

Nearby places we also serve

Several smaller places do not have their own pages but are served from the same dispatch. Merion Station and Bala Cynwyd sit next to Wynnewood. Radnor is close to Bryn Mawr. Springfield borders Broomall and Lansdowne borders Drexel Hill.

If your address is in one of these places, call as you would from anywhere else. Give the street and the cross street, and the dispatcher routes the plumber.

How dispatch decides who goes where

Dispatch works from your street address, not your ZIP code. A plumber is sent from the closest point in the field, and the dispatcher gives an honest estimate of arrival time based on traffic and the number of open calls.

In a heavy storm or a deep freeze, many homes call at once. We take emergencies first, such as active flooding and sewage backups, and schedule the rest for the same day when possible.

Reading the five town pages

Each of the five town pages is written for that place, not copied from a template. Ardmore covers apartments and shops, Bryn Mawr covers large older homes, Wynnewood follows the seasons, Broomall deals with basements and Drexel Hill deals with rowhomes and shared lines.

Start with the page closest to you. It names the ZIP codes we serve there, lists what to do before the plumber arrives and explains which municipality handles permits for the area.

What every address gets

Every address in the service area gets the same terms. The line answers 24/7, the price is stated before work starts and same-day appointments are offered when they are available. No town is treated as a second-class call.

If you live just outside the towns listed here, call anyway. The dispatcher can tell you whether we can reach you and how long it is likely to take.

The neighborhoods inside Haverford Township

Five neighborhoods inside the township have their own guides: Brookline, Oakmont, Llanerch, Manoa and Beechwood. Others, such as Chatham Park and Bon Air, are part of our home area. The age of a development often tells a plumber what to expect before arriving.

Work in the township itself requires a Township contractor license. If you are comparing plumbers, the full service list shows what we handle and the about page explains how the dispatch works.
