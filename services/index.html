Emergency plumbing and planned plumbing in one place

Haverford 24/7 Plumbing sends licensed, insured plumbers for the problems that cannot wait and for the work you can schedule. This page lists every service we offer in Haverford, PA, and each description links to a page with the symptoms, the repair options and the questions homeowners ask most.

Roughly two thirds of our services carry emergency intent: a burst pipe, a sewer backup or a failed water heater does not respect business hours. The rest are planned jobs where you want the right plan more than the fastest truck, such as repiping, a new tankless unit or basement waterproofing.

Every service, with a plain description

Water Heater Replacement

A failed or leaking tank is replaced with a right-sized unit, often in one visit. See emergency water heater replacement for how we decide between repair and replacement.

Drain Cleaning

How to pick the right page

Start with the symptom you can see. Water on a ceiling or floor belongs to leak detection or burst pipe repair. A slow or gurgling drain is drain cleaning, and sewage in the lowest drain is a main-line problem that starts at sewer line cleaning.

If the water is hot only part of the time or not at all, begin with water heater repair, and move to replacement if the tank is leaking or the unit is old. When you are unsure, call. The dispatcher can sort the problem in a minute and tell you what to shut off first.

Pricing across every service

No two plumbing jobs cost the same, so we do not publish a price list that would be wrong for your house. What every service shares is the process: a diagnosis, then a clear price before work starts, then your approval.
