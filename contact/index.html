What to have ready when you call

A minute of preparation makes the call faster.

  1. Your addressInclude the cross street if you can, and mention a gate or alley entrance if one exists.
  2. What you see and hearSay where the water is, how fast it is moving and whether a drain is gurgling.
  3. What you have shut offTell the dispatcher about the main valve or the breaker so the plumber knows the starting point.
  4. AccessMention pets, a locked basement door or anything that might slow the plumber at the entrance.
